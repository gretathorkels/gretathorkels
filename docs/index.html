greta þorkelsdóttir is a graphic designer and essay writer from reykjavík. she works mainly with culture-adjacent small businesses and independent clients, developing album artwork, visual identities and websites. In writing, she is concerned with the thin membrane between content and viewer, preoccupied with states of saturation, opacities and semiotics; the specificity in the distance between what that thing is, what it is called, what it means and what it leaves. Her most recent text is Mediumsized, published in the collection Trophy Logic in 2026.
